## The whole strategy on one page

### Where you are now
A tailoring shop selling **labour**. The customer brings cloth, you bring time. Your income is
capped by (number of tailors × hours × stitching rate) and your rate is set by the shop next door.
In the model this is scenario **A2** — and it produces about **₹12,000/month profit** on
₹1.63 L of revenue. That is the trap. Almost every tailor in Punjab is in it.

### Where you are going
A **"cloth + fit" house** — one roof where a customer picks fabric off your roll, gets measured,
gets two fittings, and walks out in a garment no one else in Jalandhar owns. Then the same fabric
stock that fills your own tailoring order book is sold **by the metre to other tailors, boutiques
and shops across Doaba**, and eventually **branded and shipped outside Punjab**.

### Why this works (the four structural advantages you get)

1. **You capture margin twice.** Retail fabric margin ~35–45% *plus* stitching margin, on the
   same customer, in the same bill. A cloth shop cannot sell the fit; a tailor cannot sell the cloth.
2. **Fabric is inventory; labour is not.** Tailoring revenue stops when you close the shutter.
   A stocked fabric counter sells on Sunday, sells to a walk-in at 8 pm, and can be sold wholesale
   in 500-metre lots without a single extra tailor.
3. **You become the supplier of your own competitors.** Every tailor in Jalandhar who buys cloth
   from you is a customer whose business grows your business. That is the distribution moat.
4. **Punjab is the right state to do this in.** Ludhiana (India's woolen/knitwear capital,
   ~50 km away) makes 95% of India's woollen knitwear; Punjab has 1.46 lakh textile units and
   10+ lakh textile workers; the state has just notified three dedicated textile policies
   (Spinning & Weaving, Dyeing & Finishing, Apparel & Technical Textiles) and an
   **Industrial & Business Development Policy 2026** with capital subsidy up to 20% of fixed
   investment and up to 75% SGST reimbursement. You are sitting on top of the supply chain.

### The three stages

| Stage | What you run | Capital | Monthly revenue (steady) | EBITDA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Premium made-to-measure tailor studio | ~₹4.7 L | ₹2.8–6 L | 26–49% |
| **B** | **+ fabric retail counter** (cloth rolls by the metre) | +₹9.7 L | +₹3.5–7 L | 10–25% |
| **C** | **+ wholesale/distribution** to tailors & shops | +₹22 L | +₹23 L → ₹2 Cr+ | 8–14% |

Stage A is where the **margin** is. Stage B is where the **customer** is. Stage C is where the
**scale** is. Do them in that order — never the reverse. The most common fatal mistake is
opening a big cloth shop before you have a reputation for fit, because then you are just another
fabric retailer competing on price with 200 shops in Sadar Bazar.

### The one-sentence version

> **Stop selling stitching. Start selling "the best-fitting suit in Jalandhar, cloth included,
> in 10 days, guaranteed" — then sell the cloth that makes it to everyone else.**

## The 10 rungs (summary — full detail in [`01-the-ladder.md`](01-the-ladder.md))

| Rung | Name | Done when… |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Fix the foundation** | Written measurement chart for every customer; standard price list; delivery promise you actually keep |
| 2 | **Reposition as a studio, not a shop** | Name, board, interior, trial room, "made-to-measure" language, 3-tier price ladder |
| 3 | **Productise & price properly** | Good/Better/Best packages; fabric+stitching bundles; no more "jo man kare wo rate" |
| 4 | **Build the fabric offer** (your gap) | First stock of 1,200–1,500 m across 8 categories; swatch books; per-metre + per-suit-length pricing |
| 5 | **Demand engine** | 45+ Google reviews, 3 Instagram posts/week, WhatsApp broadcast lists, 5 wedding-vendor tie-ups |
| 6 | **Systemise the floor** | Cutter → tailor → finisher → QC → press → pack flow with a job card that never leaves the garment |
| 7 | **Team & skills** | Master cutter you cannot lose, paid trial fittings, cross-trained tailors, written incentive plan |
| 8 | **Institutionalise** | Current account, GST, Udyam, bookkeeping, monthly P&L, insurance, bank relationship |
| 9 | **Go B2B & wholesale** | Uniform contracts, tailor-network supply, godown, field sales, credit policy |
| 10 | **Brand, multi-city, export** | Own fabric brand, second showroom (Ludhiana/Chandigarh), online D2C, NRI/export orders |

**Rungs 1–3 cost almost nothing and produce the largest profit jump in the whole plan.** Do not
buy a single metre of cloth until rung 3 is finished.

## This week (9–16 November 2026) — 7 things, no excuses

1. **Write your price list.** On paper. Three tiers. Stick it behind the counter. (Rung 3)
2. **Print 200 measurement cards** and start one for every customer who walks in — even the
   ones who don't order today. This list is your single most valuable asset. (Rung 1)
3. **Change one sentence in how you talk:** from "*ki silna hai?*" to
   "*suit banauna hai? cloth vi assi kol hi hai — 10 din vich, fitting guarantee.*" (Rung 2)
4. **Book one day for Ludhiana** — Gandhi Nagar / Ganesh Market / Chaura Bazar–Ghas Mandi —
   with ₹25,000 cash and a notebook. Buy **samples only**, not stock. (Rung 4)
5. **Ask 10 old customers for a Google review.** Send them the link on WhatsApp. (Rung 5)
6. **Call 3 wedding vendors** (makeup artist, tent house, photographer, banquet) and offer a
   referral commission. (Rung 5)
7. **Open a separate notebook for business money** — every rupee in, every rupee out, with your
   own salary written as an expense. (Rung 8)

Everything else in this plan is a consequence of those seven.

## Honest disclaimers

- Prices, rents and margins here are **planning assumptions** built from published Indian
  wholesale rate lists (Surat/Delhi/Ludhiana), boutique pricing benchmarks and typical Punjab
  shop economics. **Verify every rate on the ground before you spend money.** Jalandhar rents
  and Ludhiana fabric rates move.
- GST rates quoted (tailoring 5%, fabric/garments ≤₹2,500 5%, above ₹2,500 18%) reflect the
  **GST 2.0 changes effective 22 September 2025**. Confirm with a CA before invoicing — slabs change.
- Government scheme names, limits and Punjab policy incentives are accurate as at November 2026
  but are administered by humans; the eligibility is decided by the bank/officer, not by this document.
- This is a business plan, not financial or legal advice.
