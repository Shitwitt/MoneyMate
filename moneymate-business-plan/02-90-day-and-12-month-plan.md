## If you only have ₹50,000 to start

Do this and nothing else:
1. ₹3,000 — two suiting **swatch books** from Ludhiana (60–120 designs you can sell instantly).
2. ₹5,000 — 30 m of the 6 fastest-selling shades (navy, black, charcoal, grey, beige, olive) as
   display stock in your existing shop.
3. ₹2,000 — printed rate board + bundle cards + measurement cards.
4. ₹15,000 — shop paint, lighting, a mirror, a trial curtain, a sofa cover. Perception.
5. ₹10,000 — Instagram/Google push + 500 WhatsApp messages to old customers.
6. ₹15,000 — keep as cash for the fabric you'll buy *after* a customer pays an advance.

That is a complete Rung 1–4 start at ₹50,000, with zero inventory risk.
