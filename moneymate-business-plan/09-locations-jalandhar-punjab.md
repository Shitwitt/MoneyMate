# 09 · Where — Jalandhar, Punjab, and Beyond

"*How to start and make it a big business in Jalandhar, Punjab — and where?*"

Three separate "where" questions, three answers:
1. **Where in Jalandhar should the shop be?** (§1–3)
2. **Where do I buy the cloth?** (§4)
3. **Where do I grow next?** (§5–6)

---

## 1. First, understand your market

| Fact | Number | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|---|
| Jalandhar city population | ~8.7 lakh (Census 2011); ~11–12 lakh estimated now | Big enough to support a premium niche; small enough that reputation travels fast |
| Jalandhar district | ~21.9 lakh (2011); ~27 lakh estimated | Your real catchment, not just the city |
| Rank in Punjab | 3rd largest city (after Ludhiana, Amritsar); **largest in Doaba** | You are the commercial centre of a 4-district region |
| Language | Punjabi 81.5%, Hindi 15.6% | Sell in Punjabi first. Always. |
| Religion (city) | Hindu ~75%, Sikh ~21% | Both communities buy heavily for weddings; Sikh families add turban/safsa, Pathani, and Gurpurab buying |
| Connectivity | On NH-1 (Grand Trunk Road), major rail junction | Ludhiana 50 km, Amritsar 110 km, Chandigarh 150 km, Delhi 350 km — sourcing and distribution are easy |
| Economy | Sports goods (India's sports-goods city), leather complex, hand tools, agriculture trade, education hub, **very high NRI remittance** | Purchasing power is higher than the per-capita numbers suggest |
| Doaba NRI density | Among the highest in India — Canada (Brampton, Surrey, Calgary), UK (Southall, Birmingham), Australia, USA, UAE | **Your highest-ticket customer segment lives abroad and shops in Jalandhar for 4 weeks a year** |
| Punjab textile base | ~1.46 lakh textile units, 10+ lakh workers, ~14% of the state's manufacturing GVA, exports ~₹12,241 Cr; Punjab makes ~95% of India's woollen knitwear | You are 50 km from the densest textile supply chain in North India |

**Strategic conclusion:** Jalandhar is a **premium-capable, festival-driven, NRI-boosted,
supply-chain-adjacent** market. Do not build a cheap tailoring shop here — Ludhiana and Delhi will
undercut you on price forever. Build the **fit-and-fabric authority of Doaba**.

## 2. Choosing your location in Jalandhar

### The two business types need two different locations

| | Tailoring studio | Fabric retail counter | Fabric wholesale godown |
|---|---|---|---|
| Needs | Trust, quiet, appointment-based, parking | **Visibility, footfall, frontage, display width** | Cheap space, truck access, no frontage |
| Can hide in a lane? | **Yes** — 1st floor even works | **No** | No need |
| Rent sensitivity | High (small space) | Medium (you need the frontage) | Very high (big space) |
| Best fit | Model Town / Urban Estate / near a wedding market | A visible main road with a wide board | Basti Sheikh / Nakodar Road / Focal Point side / outskirts |

### Candidate areas — score each against §3

**Premium / high-ticket retail**
- **Model Town (Market, Main Market)** — Jalandhar's established premium shopping belt; family
  money, wedding buying, salaried professionals. Highest rent, highest ticket.
- **Urban Estate Phase I/II & Sarabha Nagar** — residential-premium; good for an appointment-based
  studio with parking; lower footfall, higher loyalty.
- **Civil Lines / Kutchery Road area** — officers, lawyers, doctors, banks. Excellent for formal
  suiting and corporate uniforms.
- **Green Park / Colonel Avenue / Mitha Bazar fringe** — mixed premium.

**High-footfall traditional markets (fabric & ethnic)**
- **Sadar Bazar** — the city's central bazaar; enormous footfall; heavy competition; rents high;
  parking terrible. Good for volume fabric, bad for a bespoke studio.
- **Guru Nanak Dev Market / Mitha Bazar / Mai Hiran Gate / Katra Bazar** — old-city cloth and
  general trade; **Jyoti Chowk** is specifically noted as a fabric hub (silk, cotton, synthetics).
- **Nehru Garden Market / Bus Stand Road** — transit footfall, value buying.
- **Rama Mandi / Garha Road / Patel Nagar** — dense residential; good for a neighbourhood
  fabric + tailoring combo, low rent, high repeat.

**Growth corridors (cheaper now, better in 3 years)**
- **Nakodar Road, Lohian Khas Road, Kartarpur Road, Hoshiarpur Road, Phagwara Road (GT Road side)**
  — main-road frontage at 40–60% of Model Town rent, with car parking. **This is where a fabric
  showroom + godown combo makes the most sense.**
- **Near Lovely Professional University (Phagwara, ~30 km)** — a student city with constant demand
  for blazers, event wear, kurta sets and cheap-formal. A satellite studio here is a real option.

### Areas to avoid for a fabric retail shop
- ❌ Deep inside a lane with no board visibility (fabric is a visual purchase)
- ❌ Purely residential lanes with no through traffic
- ❌ Next to a dominant 40-year-old cloth shop unless you have a clearly different offer
  (your different offer = **fabric + stitching + guarantee**. That's actually strong.)
- ❌ A location whose rent is above 5% of your realistic monthly revenue

## 3. The location scorecard — fill this in for every shop you visit

Score 1–5 on each. **Do not take a shop scoring below 32/50.**

| 6 | Shop width (12 ft+ = 5) and depth | ×2 | | |

| 8 | Landlord terms: 3-yr lock-in with 1-yr exit, escalation ≤10%, permission for signage & interiors | ×2 | | |
| 9 | Competition within 500 m (5 = none/weak, 1 = 5 strong shops) | ×1 | | |
| 10 | Safety, lighting at night, and the area's 5-year direction of travel | ×1 | | |
| 11 | Distance from your home (you will be there 12 hrs/day) | ×1 | | |
| | **TOTAL (max 120)** | | | |

**Field work before signing:**
1. Stand outside the shop for 30 minutes at 11 am, 6 pm and on a Sunday. **Count** people who stop
   to look at the board, and count how many walk in.
2. Visit the 3 nearest competing cloth/tailor shops as a customer. Note their rates, their display,
   their staff behaviour, how busy they are.
3. Ask 5 neighbouring shopkeepers: "ehthe kinni vikri hundi hai?" and "picchli dukaan kyun band hoi?"
4. Check with the MC about trade licence and signage permission **before** you pay the advance.
5. Verify the landlord owns the property (electricity bill / property tax receipt / registry).

## 4. Where to buy the cloth (sourcing map)

| Hub | Distance from Jalandhar | Best for | When to go |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ludhiana** — Gandhi Nagar / Ganesh Market / Hukam Singh Road; Chaura Bazar / Ghas Mandi / Ghora Gali / Dr. Gujjarmal Road; Field Ganj; Mali Ganj; Focal Point | **50 km / 1 hr** | Suiting, shirting, knits, hosiery, woolens, ready-made, manufacturers for private label | **Every 1–2 weeks in year 1.** Your primary source |
| **Amritsar** — Guru Bazaar (2,000+ shops), Shastri Market (textile hub), Hall Bazaar, Katra Jaimal Singh, Lahori Gate, Lawrence Road | 110 km / 2 hr | Ladies' ethnic, Phulkari, Patiala salwar suits, shawls, juttis, bridal | Monthly, if you add a ladies' line |
| **Delhi NCR** — Gandhi Nagar (Asia's largest readymade/textile wholesale, ~14,000 shops); Chandni Chowk & Katra Neel (ethnic/bridal/cut pieces); Sadar Bazar (trims, packaging); Naraina/Okhla (job lots) | 350 km / 5.5 hr or overnight train | Readymade garments, uniform fabric, bulk shirting, trims and packaging, bridal fabric | Every 4–6 weeks |
| **Surat (Gujarat)** — Ring Road / Sahara Darwaja / New Bombay Market / Umarwada | 1,500 km / flight or 24 hr rail | Synthetics (~40% of India's synthetic fabric), suiting/shirting at the sharpest rates, ethnic, embroidery, velvet, own-label manufacturing | From year 2, at 500 m+ per design |
| **Panipat (Haryana)** | 220 km | Blankets, shawls, home furnishing, woolens, recycled-fibre cloth | Winter sourcing |
| **Bhilwara / Jaipur (Rajasthan)** | 550–600 km | Suiting manufacturing belt, cotton prints, Jaipuri block print | Year 2+ |
| **Mumbai** — Mangaldas Market, Dadar, Kalbadevi | 1,700 km | Shirting/suiting, mill ends | Year 2+ |
| **Tirupur / Coimbatore (TN)** | — | Cotton shirting, knits, hosiery at scale | Year 3+, or via Ludhiana traders |
| **Kolkata — Burrabazar** | — | Cheap ethnic + suiting | Only if you serve that market |
| **IndiaMART / TradeIndia / Udaan / Fabriclore (online B2B)** | — | Price discovery, quotes, samples | Continuously — use to benchmark Ludhiana rates |
| **Jalandhar local** — Jyoti Chowk, Sadar Bazar, GND Market, Mitha Bazar | 0 km | Emergency top-up, benchmarking | Only when you have no choice |

**The rule:** every level closer to the mill you go, your margin rises 5–10 points. Ludhiana is one
level; Surat/Delhi wholesale is two; a mill's own distributorship is three. **Plan to move up one
level every 12 months.**

## 5. Where to grow — the expansion map

### Ring 1 (0–25 km) — months 1–18: own your own city and its tehsils
Jalandhar city → Phagwara → Kapurthala → Sultanpur Lodhi → Nakodar → Nurmahal → Kartarpur →
Phillaur → Lohian Khas → Bhogpur → Adampur → Shahkot → Mehatpur.
**Method:** van route + 2 field sales boys + weekly trade calls. This is the cheapest growth you
will ever have.

### Ring 2 (25–120 km) — months 12–30: own Doaba and reach into Malwa/Majha
Hoshiarpur → Tanda → Dasuya → Garhshankar → Nawanshahr (SBS Nagar) → Banga → Rahon → Balachaur →
Moga → Barnala → Ludhiana (trade only, not retail — it's a manufacturer's city) → Amritsar
peri-urban → Batala → Pathankot.
**Method:** add a second van route, a route supervisor, and a **satellite studio** in Hoshiarpur or
Kapurthala (cheap rent, first-mover advantage, fed from your Jalandhar godown).

### Ring 3 (Punjab + NCR + Himachal) — months 24–48
**Chandigarh / Mohali / Panchkula** (premium retail — higher rent, higher ticket, better brand
halo), **Delhi NCR** (huge Punjabi population; a distribution office, not a shop), **Shimla /
Solan / Una / Hamirpur** (Himachal's winter suiting demand is strong and under-served),
**Jammu / Kathua** (winter + wedding market).
**Method:** appoint sub-stockists/dealers on a territory-exclusive basis rather than opening shops.
Capital-light, and it builds a distribution asset you can later sell or franchise.

### Ring 4 (national + diaspora) — months 36+
- **Own-brand distribution** to 200+ retailers across North India.
- **D2C online** (Instagram shop + website) with remote measurement.
- **NRI program**: one trip a year to **Brampton & Surrey (Canada)**, one to **Southall &
  Birmingham (UK)**, plus **Melbourne/Sydney** and **Dubai/Sharjah**. Jalandhar's diaspora ties
  make this a home-ground advantage, not an export adventure.
- **Merchant exporting** of stitched garments via AEPC membership + IEC + RoSCTL/RoDTEP rebates,
  starting with UAE/UK/Canada buyers who serve the same diaspora.

### The expansion discipline
> **Never open the next location until the current one has run profitably for 6 consecutive months
> without you physically in it.** Every tailoring business that died while expanding died because
> the owner was in shop #2 while shop #1 quietly fell apart.

## 6. Where the money is, season by season (Punjab retail calendar)

| Month | Festival / event | What sells | Stock up by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Jan** | Lohri (13 Jan), Republic Day, winter weddings resume | Heavy suiting, shawls, blazers, wool; school uniform tenders | Dec |
| **Feb** | Vasant Panchami (11 Feb 2027), spring weddings | Light suiting, sherwanis, ladies' ethnic | Jan |
| **Mar** | Holi, session-end, summer arrival | Cotton shirting, linen, kurta fabric | Feb |
| **Apr** | **Baisakhi (13 Apr)** — major Punjabi buying day; summer weddings; Akshaya Tritiya (8 May 2027) | Pathani suits, kurtas, new clothes for the whole family, light suiting | Mar |
| **May–Jun** | Peak summer weddings; school uniforms for the new session | Linen, cotton, uniform fabric — **your best B2B window** | Apr |
| **Jul** | Monsoon; **Raksha Bandhan** ("rakhi te veer nu kurta" — shirts, kurtas, gift packs) | Gifting garments, cotton | Jun |
| **Aug** | Independence Day; Karwa Chauth prep begins | Ethnic for ladies, formal for men | Jul |
| **Sep** | **Diwali stocking month — the largest fabric-buying month of the year in Punjab** | Everything. Silk, jacquard, festive ethnic, suiting | **Early Aug** |
| **Oct** | Navratri → Karwa Chauth (late Oct) → **Diwali (8 Nov 2026 / ~Oct 2027)** → season bookings open | Festive wear, wedding bookings, groom packages | Sep |
| **Nov** | **Wedding season opens 20 Nov; Gurpurab 24 Nov 2026** | Sherwanis, suits, groomsmen packages, velvet, heavy silk, turban fabric | **Oct — no later** |
| **Dec** | Peak weddings to 12 Dec; Kharmas from 16 Dec; Christmas/New Year party wear | Party wear, blazers; then a hard stop | Nov |

**Two dates that decide your year:** **20 September** (is your Diwali/wedding stock in?) and
**20 October** (is your order book full for the season?). Put both on your calendar now with an alarm.

## 7. Site-visit day plan (copy this for your next Ludhiana/Jalandhar trip)

```
06:00  Leave Jalandhar for Ludhiana (own car / bus)
07:30  Breakfast near Gandhi Nagar; review your supplier spreadsheet
08:30  Shop 1–6 (Gandhi Nagar / Ganesh Market) — shirting, knits, readymade
11:00  Shop 7–12 (Chaura Bazar / Ghas Mandi / Ghora Gali) — suiting, shirting wholesalers
13:00  Lunch; update spreadsheet with all rates collected so far
14:00  Shop 13–20 — fill gaps (lining, trims, wedding fabric, ladies)
16:30  Revisit top 3 shortlisted suppliers — negotiate, take samples, agree terms
18:00  Buy 1 trial than from the best one (test order, not a big order)
18:30  Drive back; voice-note your learnings while they're fresh
21:00  Spreadsheet updated: supplier, design, composition, GSM, width, per-m rate,
       per-than rate, MOQ, credit terms, replacement policy, GSTIN, contact person
```

Bring: cash ₹25,000–50,000, a digital weighing scale (for the GSM test), a measuring tape, a
lighter (burn test), a notebook, your phone for photos, and your **existing shop's sales data** so
you can say "eh design meri dukaan te chalda hai".
