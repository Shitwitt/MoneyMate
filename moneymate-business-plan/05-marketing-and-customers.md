# 05 · Marketing and Customers — Jalandhar Edition

Marketing for a tailor shop in Punjab is not advertising. It is **proof + presence + reminders**.
Nobody buys a ₹25,000 sherwani because they saw an ad; they buy it because someone they trust
said "*ohde kol silva lai, fit bilkul perfect aa*" and because your Instagram showed them it's real.

Budget guidance: **5–8% of revenue**, ₹8,000–25,000/month at your scale.

---

## 1. Google Business Profile — your highest-ROI asset (do it today, free)

Why: in Jalandhar, "best tailor near me", "suit stitching Jalandhar", "sherwani Jalandhar",
"cloth shop Model Town" get searched daily by exactly the customers you want — including NRIs
planning a wedding from Canada and people relocating to the city.

**Setup checklist**
1. Claim the profile at `business.google.com` with the shop's phone number.
2. Business name: **your brand name only** — don't stuff keywords ("Best Tailor Jalandhar
   Cheap Rate") — Google suspends those.
3. Categories: primary **"Tailor"**; secondary **"Fabric store"**, "Men's clothing store",
   "Bridal shop", "Clothing alteration service".
4. Address + a **pin dropped exactly right** (Punjab pins are often 300 m off — fix it).
5. Hours, phone, WhatsApp link, website/Instagram link.
6. Attributes: "LGBTQ+ friendly" isn't relevant, but **"On-site services" / "Home visits"** and
   **"Appointment required"** are — turn those on.
7. **15+ photos**: board, exterior, interior, fabric wall, cutting table, trial room, 8 finished
   garments on real people. Re-upload 3 new photos a month.
8. **Services list with prices** — Google lets you list "2-piece suit — ₹3,500" etc. Do it.
9. Posts: one post a week (new fabric arrival, wedding season open, festive offer).

**Reviews — the engine**
- Target: **45 reviews in 90 days, 200 by end of year 2, 4.8+ stars.**
- The only reliable way to get reviews: **ask at the moment of delivery, with your phone in your
  hand, opening the link for them.** Conversion of the ask is ~60%; conversion of a "please review
  us" WhatsApp a week later is ~5%.
- Give the customer a **printed card with a QR code** to your review link. Keep 500 in the drawer.
- **Reply to every review** in Punjabi/English within 24 hours. Replies are read by the next
  100 customers, not by the reviewer.
- A 1-star review: call within 2 hours, fix it free, ask them to update the review. Most do.

**Local SEO beyond Google**
- Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART (IndiaMART matters hugely once you sell fabric wholesale —
  buyers actively search "suiting fabric wholesaler Punjab" there), TradeIndia, Yelp.
- Consistent **NAP** (Name, Address, Phone) across all of them. Inconsistency kills rankings.

## 2. Instagram — where Punjab's wedding market actually lives

Punjab is one of India's most Instagram-dense states. Wedding content travels through it faster
than any paid ad.

**Account structure**
- Handle = your brand name, no underscores/numbers if possible.
- Bio: `Made-to-measure suits · Sherwanis · Fabric by the metre 📍 Model Town, Jalandhar
  🚚 Home measurement · ✈️ NRI orders · 📲 WhatsApp link`
- **Highlights:** Suits · Sherwanis · Fabric · Fittings · Reviews · Rates · Location.
- Link in bio → a WhatsApp click-to-chat link (`wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX?text=...`).

**The 7 content formats that work (rotate weekly)**

| # | Format | Example | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Fit transformation reel** | Ill-fitting readymade jacket → your bespoke fit on the same body, 15 sec, before/after cut | The single highest-performing format in tailoring |
| 2 | **Cutting-room footage** | Chalk, shears, canvas, hand-pad stitching, ASMR | Signals craft; justifies price |
| 3 | **Fabric education** | "How to tell real wool from polyester in 10 seconds (burn test)", "GSM kya hota hai" | Positions you as the expert, not the salesman |
| 4 | **Wedding lookbook** | Groom + groomsmen colour story, one wedding, 8 photos | Sells the ₹2–6 L package, not the ₹6,000 suit |
| 5 | **Customer testimonial** | 20-second Punjabi clip at delivery | Trust transfer |
| 6 | **Rate transparency** | "Jalandhar vich suit silai de rate — 3 tier vich dasde haan" | Radical transparency = saves + shares |
| 7 | **Behind the season** | Rush week, 40 orders, team at 10 pm | Humanises; creates urgency |

**Cadence:** 3 reels + 2 carousels + daily stories. Punjabi captions with English hashtags.
**Hashtags:** #jalandhar #jalandharpunjab #punjabiwedding #sherwani #madetomeasure #bespokesuit
#doaba #punjabifashion #groomwear #suiting #ludhiana #chandigarh #nriwedding #punjabiweddingdress
**Music:** trending Punjabi tracks (they carry the algorithm here).

**Free growth hacks**
- Tag the **makeup artist, photographer, venue and decorator** in every wedding post. Their
  followers are your customers, and they reshare.
- Comment meaningfully on 20 local business/wedding pages a day.
- Run a **"tag your groom" contest** in October before the season.
- Reels of **turban/safsa tying** for grooms pull enormous Punjabi engagement and are directly
  adjacent to your product.

**Paid ads (only after 500 followers and 20 posts)**
- ₹3,000–8,000/month, **Instagram + Facebook**, objective = WhatsApp messages (not likes).
- Geo: Jalandhar + 25 km radius (Phagwara, Kapurthala, Hoshiarpur, Nawanshahr, Nakodar, Kartarpur).
  Separate campaign: **NRI audience** — Brampton, Surrey, Vancouver, Calgary, Southall, Birmingham,
  Melbourne, Dubai — targeting Punjabi language + "recently in India" interest proxies.
- Creative: a fit-transformation reel + "Book your wedding season slot". CTA: WhatsApp.
- Expect ₹40–150 per WhatsApp conversation in Jalandhar; ₹300–900 per NRI conversation. One
  NRI groom order (₹80,000) pays for a year of ads.

## 3. WhatsApp Business — your CRM, free

1. Install **WhatsApp Business**, register the shop number, add catalogue with your price tiers,
   set a greeting message and **quick replies** (`/rates`, `/delivery`, `/address`, `/fabric`).
2. **Broadcast lists** (max 256 each), segmented:
   - `Grooms-2026-27` (anyone who enquired about a wedding)
   - `Repeat-2yr` (ordered in the last 2 years)
   - `NRI` (Canada/UK/Aus/UAE numbers)
   - `Trade-Tailors` (B2B — wholesale rate lists only)
   - `VIP` (top 50 by lifetime value)
3. **Status daily** in season: new fabric arrival, a delivered garment, "3 slots left this week".
   Status is the most under-used selling tool in Punjab — people watch it silently and then call.
4. **Reminder messages that feel personal, not bulk:**
   > "Satinder ji, pichle saal November vich tuhadi navy suit silwai si. Eh saal winter suiting
   > aa gayi aa — wool blend, 12 naven designs. Vekhan aao? Ya main photo bhej dawan?"
   Send this to 100 people on 20 October. Expect 12–20 orders.
5. **Never spam.** Two promotional messages a month is the ceiling. Third one gets you blocked.

## 4. The wedding ecosystem — 10 partnerships that fill your calendar

A Punjabi wedding is a supply chain of 15–20 vendors. Every one of them meets your customer
**before** you do. Pay them properly and they become your sales force.

| Partner | Why | Offer |
|---|---|---|
| **Makeup artist / beautician** | Meets the bride and her whole family, 5–8 functions | 10% referral or a free stitched suit/blouse |
| **Wedding photographer / videographer** | Knows every wedding in the city 6 months ahead | 10% + you refer clients back to them |
| **Tent house / decorator** | Books first, often takes the whole family's contact | 8–10% |
| **Banquet / marriage palace manager** | Sees 3–6 families a week | 10% + a Diwali gift |
| **Wedding planner / event company** | Controls the groom's look budget directly | 12–15% |
| **Jeweller** | Same customer, same month, high ticket | Mutual referral, no cash |
| **Gurdwara / pandit / wedding-date astrologer** | Knows the muhurat list before anyone | Respect + a donation, not a commission |
| **Barber salon / men's grooming** | Men talk suits at the barber | 8% + a rate card at their mirror |
| **Shoe shop (formal) + turban/safsa specialist** | Completes the groom look | Bundle: "groom package" cross-sell |
| **Travel agent / visa consultant** | **NRIs!** They know who is flying in and when | 10% per NRI order — this one is worth its weight in gold in Doaba |

**How to close a partnership (do it in person, not on the phone):**
1. Visit with a garment you made and your rate card.
2. Ask for nothing at first — offer: *"Tuhade clients nu koi suit chahida hove tan dasna, assi
   ghar aa ke naap le javange. Tuhada 10% commission, cash, har delivery ton baad."*
3. Deliver a printed referral slip pad with their name on it.
4. **Pay the commission on the same day as delivery**, in cash or UPI. Speed of payment is the
   whole relationship.
5. Diwali gift + a free garment for the partner themselves. This costs ₹3,000 and returns ₹2 L.

**Target: 10 active partners by 31 January 2027, each producing 2+ orders a month.**

## 5. The NRI channel — Doaba's unfair advantage

Jalandhar sits in the Doaba, one of the highest-emigration regions in India. NRI families come
home for 3–6 weeks for weddings and want to buy **everything** in that window, in cash/card,
at Indian prices, with quality they can't get in Brampton or Southall.

**The NRI product:**
- **Pre-arrival**: video-call consultation, courier/WhatsApp **swatch photos and fabric video**,
  a written style sheet, a slot reserved against a 30% advance paid by bank transfer/UPI.
- **In-India**: all measurements taken in **one visit** (or a home/hotel visit — offer it free),
  3 fittings compressed into 12 days, delivery to their family home.
- **Post-return**: courier of finished garments to Canada/UK/Australia with **DHL/FedEx**
  (₹3,500–7,000 per shipment, 4–7 days) — charge it, don't absorb it.
- **Reorder**: their measurements are in your database forever. "Send me a photo of the fabric
  you want" becomes a ₹40,000 order from Surrey in February.

**Where to find them:**
1. Instagram/Facebook ads geo-targeted at Brampton, Surrey, Vancouver, Calgary, Edmonton,
   Southall, Wolverhampton, Birmingham, Melbourne, Sydney, Dubai, Sharjah — Punjabi-language creative.
2. **Visa/travel consultants and ticketing agents** in Jalandhar (see partnership table).
3. NRI WhatsApp groups (family groups, village associations, sangeet planning groups) — get
   introduced by a satisfied customer, never cold-join.
4. **Wedding planners who handle NRI weddings** — they need a reliable local tailor desperately.
5. Gurdwara committees and Punjabi cultural associations abroad — sponsor a small event.

**Pricing:** NRI customers pay **20–40% above your local rate** without blinking if the service is
flawless and communication is fast. Never quote them your local rate.

## 6. B2B and institutional marketing (uniforms & bulk)

This is a **door-knocking and relationship** business, not an advertising one.

**Target list to build in Jalandhar district:**
- Private schools & convent schools (hundreds; each orders annually, Mar–Jun)
- Colleges, coaching institutes (Aakash/Allen-type centres), polytechnics, universities (LPU is
  30 km away in Phagwara — a giant for blazers and event wear)
- Hospitals, nursing homes, diagnostic labs (scrubs, OT dresses, staff coats)
- Hotels, restaurants, banquet staff
- Factories in Jalandhar Focal Point, Udyog Nagar, and the Leather Complex (workwear)
- Security agencies, gyms, sports academies (Jalandhar is India's sports-goods city)
- Banks, insurance offices, car showrooms, real-estate offices (formal shirts/blazers)
- Gurdwara committees (sevadars, langar staff), religious trusts, event volunteers
- Political offices and election campaigns (huge, seasonal, high-volume, low-margin — take it
  only with 100% advance)

**The pitch that wins:**
> "Aapke staff ki 12 measurements hum ek din mein, aapke office mein aa kar le lenge. Rate ₹X
> per shirt, 2 sets per person, GST invoice, 45-day delivery, aur har 6 mahine mein free
> alteration. Ek sample pehle bhej rahe hain — check kar lijiye."

**Always send a free sample garment first.** In institutional sales, the sample is the sales pitch.

**Payment terms:** 50% advance, 50% on delivery for private; follow government GFR/GeM rules for
public bodies (register on **GeM** — the Government e-Marketplace — it's where a lot of uniform
tendering happens now).

## 8. Retention — the cheapest revenue you will ever buy

- **Repeat rate target: 45% by year 2, 60% by year 3.**
- **The 3-month rule:** every customer gets a personal WhatsApp at +3 months
  ("shirt chahidi hai us suit de naal?") and +9 months ("winter aa gaya, blazer?").
- **The family rule:** after a groom order, call the father, the brothers, and the *bride's*
  family for their needs. One wedding = 13 potential orders.
- **The birthday/Diwali rule:** Diwali gift to your top 50 (a box of sweets + a fabric swatch
  card). Cost ₹15,000. Return: the entire next season.
- **Loyalty:** 5th garment gets a free shirt, or 5% off fabric. Simple, printed on the bill.
- **The guarantee:** *"Fit na aave tan free alteration, ya paisa wapas"* — printed on the board.
  You will almost never have to refund, but it removes the biggest purchase objection there is.

## 10. The one metric that matters

**Cost per *delivered order* by source.** Track it in a single column on your order form
("How did you hear about us?"). At the end of every month:

| Source | Orders | Cost | Cost/order |
|---|---|---|---|
| Referral / word of mouth | ? | ₹0 | ₹0 |
| Google reviews/profile | ? | ₹0 | ₹0 |
| Instagram organic | ? | time | ~₹0 |
| Instagram/FB ads | ? | ₹6,000 | ? |
| Vendor partners | ? | commissions | ? |
| Walk-in | ? | rent | ? |
| WhatsApp database | ? | ₹0 | ₹0 |

Kill whatever costs more than **8% of average order value**. Double whatever is under 3%.
In a city like Jalandhar, referral + Google + WhatsApp will be 70% of your business for years.
That is not a weakness — it is the moat.
