## T8 · Fabric stock register (spreadsheet columns)

```
Roll code | Supplier | GST invoice no | Date in | Design / composition | GSM | Width |
Shrinkage % | Metres purchased | Landed ₹/m | Total cost | Wholesale ₹/m | Retail ₹/m |
Rack / bin | Opening metres | Issued (job card no) | Sold retail (m) | Sold wholesale (m) |
Balance metres | Last movement date | Days idle | Status (Active / Slow / Clearance)
```

Rules:
- Balance must be physically verified weekly for the top 20 rolls by value, monthly for all.
- `Days idle > 90` → move to Clearance automatically.
- `Days idle > 180` → cut into sample books and give them to trade buyers.
