**This table is the most important page in the whole plan.** A2 (the typical Jalandhar tailor shop) barely clears its own salaries — the owner works 12 hours and takes home less than a bank clerk. A3 earns 4–6× more from *fewer* garments because the charge per unit carries the design consultation, the fabric, the trial and the guarantee. The move from A2 to A3 is not a skill upgrade, it is a **product, pricing and shop-front upgrade** — and it is the whole ladder.

Wholesale is a **volume + credit-management** business, not a margin business. The single killer is receivables: 15% gross margin disappears if one buyer out of ten defaults. Cap credit at 30 days, cap exposure per buyer, and stop supply the day a buyer crosses the cap.

## 8. Tax snapshot used in this model

| What you sell | GST | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Tailoring / stitching on customer's fabric (SAC 9988) | 5% | Reduced from 18% under GST 2.0 effective 22 Sep 2025 |
| Woven / knitted fabric, garment up to ₹2,500 per piece | 5% | Cotton fabric, suiting, shirting all sit here |
| Garment / made-up above ₹2,500 per piece | 18% | Per-piece test, not per-bill — a premium suit crosses this |
| Fashion-design + tailoring bundled as a design service | 18% | If you sell 'design' rather than 'stitching', rate changes — ask your CA |
| Composition scheme limit (services) | ₹50 lakh turnover | Above it you must go regular; goods limit is ₹1.5 Cr (verify with CA) |
| GST registration threshold (Punjab) | ₹40 lakh goods / ₹20 lakh services | Register voluntarily earlier — B2B buyers want a GST invoice |

> Confirm the current rate with your CA before printing a bill. GST slabs for apparel changed in September 2025 and can change again.

## 9. The five numbers to check every Sunday

| Number | Target | Why it kills you if it slips |
|---|---|---|
| Units stitched / tailor / day | ≥ 2.2 | Below 1.8 you cannot pay the salary |
| Average charge / unit | rising 10%/yr | Flat charge = shrinking real margin |
| Fabric stock turn | ≥ 6× a year | Dead rolls eat your working capital |
| Receivables days (wholesale) | ≤ 35 days | Above 60 days you are the bank |
| Repeat-customer share | ≥ 45% by Y2 | Below 30% your product has a problem |
